# GitPulse Mobile

GitPulse Mobile is a Flutter app for keeping up with your GitHub work on the go. It focuses on the parts that matter most when you're away from your desk: **Release Timeline**, **Release Follows**, and **repository / release details**.

---

## What You Can Do

- **Release Timeline** — See releases from the repositories you follow in one feed, newest first.
- **Release Follows** — Choose which repositories appear in your timeline.
- **Repository & Release Details** — Open a repository or release to read the full changelog and browse its assets.
- **Use the official service or your own server** — Sign in to the official GitPulse instance, or connect to a self-hosted GitPulse backend.

---

## Get Started

1. Download the latest Android build from the project's **Releases** page.
2. Open the app.
3. Sign in to the official GitPulse instance at **[gitpulse.euphony.ink](https://gitpulse.euphony.ink)**, or enter the address of your self-hosted GitPulse server.
4. Follow the repositories you care about and check your Release Timeline.

No backend setup is needed if you use the official instance.

---

## Platform Support

**Android** is the main supported platform. iOS support is included in the codebase, but it is not officially verified.
